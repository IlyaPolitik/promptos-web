Practical prompts for work that needs a second look

A useful team prompt names its inputs, exposes unknowns, and gives a person a clear review decision. Start with a recurring job below, adapt the template to your approved sources, and check the output before it reaches a customer.

These guides are examples for sales and customer teams. Their fictional illustrations are not customer evidence or claims about PromptOS outcomes.
