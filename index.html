PromptOS

Approved AI Workflows for Business Teams

PromptOS is the AI prompt workspace for business teams. Rough requests are turned into structured prompts, routed by risk, checked against team standards, and shipped as approved AI workflows with evals, approvals, run history, and evidence.

Teams use PromptOS to compare model outputs, version saved prompts, apply shared standards, and track the usage and value of AI work across the workspace — on web, desktop, mobile, and the browser extension.
